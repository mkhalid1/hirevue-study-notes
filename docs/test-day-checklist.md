# Test-day checklist

Everything to check, in order, so the only thing left to think about is your answers.

## The day before

- [ ] Reread your invitation: deadline, device advice, and whether it includes games.
- [ ] Read your STAR story notes and your "why us" sentences out loud once.
- [ ] Choose your room and check the light at the time you plan to record.
- [ ] Charge your device and test your internet speed (HireVue recommends at least 350 Kbps up and down).
- [ ] Decide what you will wear, as you would for an in-person interview with this employer.
- [ ] Stop preparing in good time and sleep.

## One hour before

- [ ] Eat something and have water within reach.
- [ ] Close other apps and tabs. Turn off notifications.
- [ ] Tell anyone you live with that you need quiet for 45 minutes.
- [ ] Put your bullet notes beside the screen, not over the camera.
- [ ] Have a pen and paper for jotting S, T, A, R during thinking time.

## In the first minutes

- [ ] Read the intro screens: number of questions, question types and estimated time.
- [ ] Note the think time, answer time and number of retakes for each question, if shown. These are set by the employer.
- [ ] Use a practice question to check light, sound and framing, and watch it back.
- [ ] Use another practice question as a warm-up if you want one. Nobody else sees them.

## During the interview

- [ ] For each question: name the competency, pick the story, jot four words, then answer.
- [ ] Spend most of each answer on what you did.
- [ ] Land a result and stop. Spare seconds are fine.
- [ ] In simulations, speak to the person, not about them.
- [ ] If a retake is allowed and your answer went badly wrong, use it. If it was decent, move on.
- [ ] In games, read every rule before starting, and keep going after a hard round.

## If something goes wrong

- [ ] Connection lost: reopen the same link. HireVue says you can pick up where you left off.
- [ ] Still stuck: contact the recruiter or hiring team. They manage the invitation and deadline.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Write down the questions you were asked while you remember them. They help you prepare for the next stage.
- [ ] Note which of your stories you used, so you can build on them in a live interview.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a day spare, do one last timed run with the [free HireVue practice on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/hirevue?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=hirevue) in the same room and on the same device.
