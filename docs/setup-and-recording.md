# Setup and recording

**In one line:** a quiet room, steady light in front of you, a stable connection and a tested microphone remove most of the things that can go wrong in a recorded interview.

Nothing on this page is scored on its own. It matters because a dark, noisy or laggy recording makes good answers harder to follow, and because technical stress uses up the attention you need for the questions.

## Your device and connection

- **Device:** HireVue says you can use a computer, tablet or phone. Choose the one you are most comfortable with. On mobile, the invitation usually opens the HireVue for Candidates app.
- **Internet:** HireVue recommends at least 350 Kbps (0.35 Mbps) for both upload and download. Most home connections are far above that, but a speed test takes a minute.
- **Power:** keep the device fully charged or plugged in.
- **Microphone:** test it before you start. The setup screens include an equipment check.
- **Notifications:** turn them off. A banner or a ringtone halfway through an answer is distracting for you and the listener.
- **Device the employer recommends:** if the invitation says to use a computer for the games, do that. HireVue's own advice for games is to use the device the employer recommends.

## Light, sound and framing

HireVue's interview tips are short and practical: find a quiet, comfortable place with the strongest Wi-Fi signal you can get, and **face your light source**. Light behind you, such as a window, turns you into a silhouette.

Our additional guidance:

- Put the camera roughly at eye level. A stack of books under a laptop works.
- Frame yourself from about mid-chest up, with a little space above your head.
- A plain or tidy background is less distracting than a busy one.
- Soft furnishings (curtains, a sofa, a bed) reduce echo. An empty room with hard walls can sound hollow.
- Wired earphones with a microphone can help in a noisy home, but test them first.

HireVue's tips also say you don't need to worry about looking into the camera. Look where feels natural, and if you have notes nearby, glance at them as you would in a live conversation.

## Practice questions

Before the real interview, HireVue gives you practice questions. From HireVue's support articles:

- There are **three generic practice questions**, and you can redo them as many times as you like.
- You can **watch your practice answers back**. This is the moment to check light, sound and framing.
- Practice answers are **never uploaded or seen by anyone else**.
- The practice questions will not match your real ones, because each employer chooses its own questions.
- Practice is available for OnDemand interviews.

Use at least one practice question purely as a tech check: play it back and listen for echo, background noise and whether your face is clearly lit. Use the next one as a warm-up for talking to a camera.

Once the real interview begins, you cannot watch your answers back.

## Before you press start

The first screens of your interview tell you the company, the role, how many questions there are, the question types and the estimated time. Read them. They are the only reliable source for your timings, because think time, answer time and retakes are set by the employer.

HireVue says most video-based assessments take under 20 to 30 minutes, and advises setting aside 45 minutes. For games, HireVue's article advises setting aside at least 20 minutes so you do not feel rushed.

## If something goes wrong

- **Connection drops:** HireVue says you can pick up where you left off, using the same link you started with.
- **The link will not open or keeps failing:** contact the employer's recruiter or hiring team. They manage your invitation and deadline.
- **You need adjustments:** HireVue's FAQ says employers may support neurodivergent candidates and people with disabilities who prefer to take an interview or assessment another way, and that you can request accommodations from your recruiter or the hiring team. If you choose to tell an employer you are neurodivergent, you may be able to ask for the interview questions in advance. Disclosure is your choice.

## Your data

HireVue's FAQ says your data is kept for a period chosen by the company you are interviewing with, then deleted automatically. If you want it deleted sooner, the request goes to that employer, usually through the recruiter or the application page, because the employer decides.

## Practise the setup

A full dry run, including light and sound, takes ten minutes. Try the [free timed HireVue practice on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/hirevue?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=62p2am&utm_content=hirevue) in the same room and on the same device you plan to use on the day.
