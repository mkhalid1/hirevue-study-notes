# HireVue explained

**In one line:** HireVue is a hiring platform that employers use to run recorded video interviews and short game-based assessments, usually as an early screening step before you speak to a person.

If you have an email invitation that says "HireVue", you are not taking a single fixed test. Each employer builds its own interview and picks its own mix of games for the role. That is why two friends who both "did a HireVue" can describe very different experiences. This page explains the parts that stay the same.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Provider | HireVue |
| Video interview type | OnDemand: recorded on your own, at a time and place you choose, with no person on the other end |
| Number of video questions | Set by the employer. HireVue says most video-based assessments have five to eight questions. |
| Time per answer | Set by the employer. HireVue says you get "several minutes to respond to each question". |
| Think time and retakes | Set by the employer and shown in your setup screens. Not published as fixed numbers. |
| Total video time | HireVue says most video-based assessments take under 20 to 30 minutes, and advises setting aside 45 minutes |
| Practice | Three generic practice questions, unlimited redos, never seen by anyone else |
| Games | A short series of games, each under 20 minutes, in three domains. Number and names of games: not published. |
| Facial recognition | HireVue says it does not use facial recognition to score candidates |
| Hiring decisions | Made by people. HireVue says the interview helps recruiters make the first cut. |
| Devices | Computer, tablet or phone. Internet of at least 350 Kbps up and down. |

Sources: [HireVue candidate FAQ](https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq), [HireVue game-based assessments](https://www.hirevue.com/platform/game-based-assessments) and [HireVue's practice FAQ](https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044807611-Practice-your-interview-FAQ), checked 24 November 2026.

## The two parts of a HireVue assessment

An employer can use one part or both.

**1. The OnDemand video interview.** You see a question, written or as a short video, and record your answer with your camera. HireVue's candidate FAQ describes three kinds of question:

- **Past-behaviour questions:** tell us about a time you did something. See [past-behaviour questions](sections/video-past-behaviour-questions.md).
- **Situational judgement questions:** what would you do if this happened. See [situational questions](sections/video-situational-questions.md).
- **Scenario-based simulations:** HireVue says the difference here is that you "act out" your response rather than explain it, for example speaking to a customer as if they were in front of you. These are covered in the same guide as situational questions.

Most interviews also include questions about why you want the job and what you are good at. See [motivation and strengths questions](sections/video-motivation-questions.md).

**2. Game-based assessments.** HireVue describes "a short series of engaging games", each under 20 minutes, that cover three competency domains:

| HireVue domain | What it covers | Guides in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| Working with Information | Problem-solving, critical thinking, decision-making | [Numerical](sections/games-numerical.md), [Memory and attention](sections/games-memory-and-attention.md), [Pattern and logic](sections/games-pattern-and-logic.md) |
| Working with People | Collaboration, communication, interpersonal skills | [People and work-style](sections/games-people-and-work-style.md) |
| Personality and Work Style | How you approach tasks and responsibilities | [People and work-style](sections/games-people-and-work-style.md) |

HireVue's own articles describe game types such as remembering sequences of letters and numbers, spotting patterns in images, working with numbers, choosing between two pictures to show your work style, and handling a customer or team situation. HireVue does not publish a list of game names or a fixed number of games, and its psychologists pick the mix for each employer. Treat any list of "the HireVue games" on other sites as unofficial.

Two more things HireVue states about the games: they are randomized, and you move up or down in levels depending on how you do. So the games get harder when you do well, and a hard round can be a sign you are doing fine.

## How the AI part works, in HireVue's words

This is the part people worry about most, so here is only what HireVue itself says:

- Each assessment model "is designed for a specific job role", and some are built for one company. There is no single model used for every job.
- The video assessment looks at job-relevant competencies. HireVue's examples include teamwork, persistence, and, for a sales role, the ability to negotiate.
- HireVue says its assessments look at the whole context of an answer, not just certain words, and never look at age, race, gender, or résumé items such as GPA or college.
- HireVue does not use facial recognition to score candidates. Some employers use separate technology to check your identity.
- The interview and assessments help recruiters make the first cut. People in the top group move on to person-to-person interviews, and hiring decisions are made by people.

HireVue's FAQ also says plainly that no course can help you "game" an AI-driven assessment. That matches the approach in these notes: you prepare the way you would for any interview, by knowing your stories and practising saying them well.

## What a typical session looks like

1. You get an email invitation with a link and a deadline.
2. The first screens show the company, the role, how many questions there are, what types they are and how long it should take.
3. You accept the terms and conditions, test your camera and microphone, and get the option to practise.
4. You answer up to three generic practice questions as many times as you like, and you can watch them back. Nobody else ever sees them.
5. You start the real interview. From here you cannot watch your answers back.
6. If the employer added games, they come as part of the same flow or as a separate link.

If your connection drops, HireVue says you can pick up where you left off using the same link.

## The one habit that runs through everything

Answer the question you were asked, with a real example, in a clear order. That single habit is what the video sections train, and the games sections train the thinking skills underneath. Nothing here is about tricks.
