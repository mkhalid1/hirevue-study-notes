# 15-minute diagnostic

Fourteen original items, two for each of the seven guides in this repo, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 12 minutes, answer everything without notes or a calculator, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from HireVue or any employer's assessment.

If you already know what kind of HireVue assessment your employer uses, you can also go straight to the [free HireVue practice on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/hirevue?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=hirevue) for a timed run.

## Part A: Past-behaviour questions

**D1.** The question is "Tell us about a time you helped a colleague." Which outline is best?

- A. "I'm always happy to help colleagues. It's one of my best qualities."
- B. "A colleague at the call centre was struggling with the new returns process. I sat with her for two calls, wrote her a short checklist, and checked in at the end of the week. Her call times dropped back to normal."
- C. "We had a busy week once and everyone helped each other."

**D2.** Which STAR part should take the most time in a 2-minute answer?

- A. Action
- B. Situation
- C. Result

## Part B: Situational questions

**D3.** "A customer becomes angry because a product they want is out of stock." Which outline is best?

- A. Tell them it is not your fault and suggest another shop.
- B. Acknowledge their frustration, check stock at other branches or the next delivery date, offer to reserve or order it, and confirm how they will be told.
- C. Call a manager straight away without speaking to the customer.

**D4.** A simulation asks you to "respond to the customer now." What should your answer sound like?

- A. "The correct procedure in this case is to..."
- B. "I would tell the customer that I'm sorry and..."
- C. "I'm sorry about this. Let me check what I can do for you."

## Part C: Motivation and strengths questions

**D5.** Which "why us" answer is strongest?

- A. "You're a market leader and I want to work somewhere successful."
- B. "Your new same-day repair service is what this role supports, and I ran the repair desk at my last job, so I'd like to help build it out."
- C. "I've applied to a lot of companies in this sector and you're one of them."

**D6.** Which is the best answer to "What is your greatest strength?"

- A. A single strength, a short example proving it, and why it matters for this job.
- B. A list of five strengths with no examples.
- C. "I don't like to talk about myself."

## Part D: Numerical games

**D7.** What is 35% of 80?

**D8.** What comes next? **1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ?**

## Part E: Memory and attention games

**D9.** Look at **P 3 W 8 L 1** for 5 seconds, then cover it. What was the second-to-last character?

**D10.** How many pairs differ? List A: **5820, 1147, 3093**. List B: **5820, 1174, 3093**.

## Part F: Pattern and logic games

**D11.** What comes next? **B, D, G, K, ?**

**D12.** Some of the drivers are trainees. All trainees wear a green badge. Must every driver wear a green badge?

## Part G: People and work-style games

**D13.** In a "which is more like you" task, what is the right approach?

- A. Pick the option you think the employer wants.
- B. Pick the option closer to how you usually work.
- C. Alternate between options to look balanced.

**D14.** A colleague says, "I've been waiting all morning for that file." Which reply is best?

- A. "Sorry for the wait. I'm sending it now, and I'll flag it to you first next time."
- B. "Everyone's busy, you know."
- C. "Well, you didn't remind me."

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

**D1: B.** A real example, your actions, a result.

**D2: A.** Action carries the evidence.

**D3: B.** Acknowledge, act, confirm.

**D4: C.** A simulation asks you to speak to the person, not describe what you would say.

**D5: B.** Specific to the employer and linked to your experience.

**D6: A.** Claim, example, relevance.

**D7: 28.** 10% is 8, so 30% is 24, and 5% is 4. 24 + 4 = 28.

**D8: 36.** These are square numbers: 6 × 6 = 36.

**D9: L.** The sequence was P, 3, W, 8, L, 1.

**D10: 1.** Only the middle pair differs (1147 and 1174).

**D11: P.** The gaps are 2, 3, 4, so the next gap is 5. K is the 11th letter, and 11 + 5 = 16, which is P.

**D12: No.** Only the trainees must wear the badge, and only some drivers are trainees.

**D13: B.** Work-style items describe you.

**D14: A.** Apologise, fix it now, prevent a repeat.

</details>
