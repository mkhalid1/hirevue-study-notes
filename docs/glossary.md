# Glossary

Plain-English meanings for terms you will meet in HireVue invitations, in these notes and in other guides.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accommodation** | A change to how you take the interview or assessment, such as more time or a different format, requested through the recruiter or hiring team. |
| **Adaptive game** | A game that gets harder or easier depending on how you are doing. HireVue says its games move you up or down levels. |
| **Answer time** | The time you have to record each answer. Set by the employer. |
| **Competency** | A job-related skill or behaviour, such as teamwork, persistence or negotiation, that an assessment looks for. |
| **Domain** | One of HireVue's three game areas: Personality and Work Style, Working with People, Working with Information. |
| **First cut** | HireVue's phrase for the early screening stage its interviews help with, before person-to-person interviews. |
| **Game-based assessment** | Short game-like tasks that measure skills such as memory, pattern spotting or work style. |
| **Hypothetical question** | A question about what you would do in an imagined situation. Also called a situational question. |
| **OnDemand interview** | HireVue's recorded video interview, done at a time and place you choose, with no person on the other end. |
| **Past-behaviour question** | A question about something you actually did, usually starting "Tell us about a time...". |
| **Practice question** | One of three generic questions HireVue lets you answer and watch back before your real interview. Not seen by anyone else. |
| **Retake** | A second attempt at recording an answer, if the employer allows it. |
| **Scenario-based simulation** | A question where you act out your response, speaking as if the person in the scenario were in front of you. |
| **STAR** | Situation, Task, Action, Result: a structure for past-behaviour answers. |
| **Story bank** | Six to eight prepared real examples you can adapt to different questions. |
| **Think time** | The time between seeing a question and recording starting. Set by the employer. |
| **Working memory** | Holding a few pieces of information in mind while you use them. Trained in the [memory and attention guide](sections/games-memory-and-attention.md). |
| **Work-style item** | A choice that describes how you like to work, such as two pictures where you pick the one more like you. There is no right answer. |
