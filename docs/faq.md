# FAQ

Real questions people search for about HireVue, answered from HireVue's own pages where a fact is involved. The first seven come from Google's "People also ask" results for "hirevue assessment" in November 2026.

## What is a HireVue interview like?

You get an email link, open it on a computer, tablet or phone, and see the company, the role, the number of questions and an estimated time. You can answer up to three generic practice questions as often as you like, then start the real interview. Each question appears as text or a short video, and you record your answer with your camera. There is nobody on the other end. HireVue calls this an OnDemand interview, because you choose when and where to do it. See [HireVue explained](test-overview.md).

## How is HireVue assessed?

HireVue says each assessment model is designed for a specific job, and it evaluates answers against competencies for that job, such as teamwork or persistence. It says it looks at the whole context of an answer, not just certain words, and does not look at age, race, gender or résumé details such as GPA. Game-based assessments cover three domains: Personality and Work Style, Working with People, and Working with Information. More detail in [how HireVue assesses you](scoring.md).

## Can you fail a HireVue interview?

There is no published pass mark. HireVue says the interview helps recruiters make the first cut, and that the top group moves on to person-to-person interviews. So an employer can decide not to move you forward, but there is no fixed score that counts as a fail.

## How to pass a HireVue assessment?

Prepare the way you would for any interview, because that is what it is. Know the job, have six to eight real stories ready in STAR form, know why you want this role, and practise saying answers out loud against a timer. HireVue's own FAQ says no course can help you "game" its assessments, and we agree. The [study plans](study-plans.md) put this into a week or two days.

## What are some common interview questions asked at HireVue?

HireVue does not set the questions. Each employer chooses its own. The common types are past-behaviour questions ("Tell us about a time..."), situational questions ("What would you do if..."), simulations where you act out a response, and motivation questions ("Why this role?"). The three video guides cover each type with worked examples: [past-behaviour](sections/video-past-behaviour-questions.md), [situational](sections/video-situational-questions.md) and [motivation and strengths](sections/video-motivation-questions.md).

## Will HireVue detect cheating?

HireVue says its customers may use identity verification technology, and it does not publish other details of how integrity is checked. The more practical point is this: the HireVue step decides who gets a live interview, and anything in your answers can come up again there. Answers you did not produce yourself tend to fall apart under follow-up questions. Use your own stories and your own work.

## Does HireVue use facial recognition?

HireVue says it does not use facial recognition to score candidates. Some employers use separate technology to verify that the person recording is you.

## Does AI decide whether I get the job?

HireVue says hiring decisions are made by people. Its assessments help recruiters make the first cut, and the top group goes on to person-to-person interviews.

## How long is a HireVue interview?

HireVue says most video-based assessments have five to eight questions and take under 20 to 30 minutes, and suggests setting aside 45 minutes. Games are each under 20 minutes. Your invitation shows the estimated time for your own interview.

## How much time do I get to think and answer, and can I retake answers?

These are set by the employer, so they vary. The setup screens show them before each question. Do not rely on numbers you read online.

## Can I practise first?

Yes. HireVue gives you three generic practice questions that you can redo as many times as you like and watch back. Nobody else ever sees them. They will not match your real questions, because each employer picks its own. For more timed practice, try the [free HireVue practice on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/hirevue?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=62p2am&utm_content=hirevue).

## Can I use notes?

HireVue's interview tips suggest writing brief stories about challenges you have faced and keeping notes nearby. Short bullet notes work far better than a script.

## What are the HireVue games?

HireVue describes a short series of games, each under 20 minutes, covering three domains. Its articles describe game types such as remembering letters and numbers, spotting patterns in images, working with numbers, choosing between two pictures to show your work style, and handling a customer situation. HireVue does not publish the names or number of games, and it sets the mix per employer. This repo has four guides on the game skills, starting with [numerical games](sections/games-numerical.md).

## Can I do it on my phone?

Yes. HireVue says you can use a computer, tablet or phone. If your invitation recommends a particular device, especially for games, use that one.

## What if my connection drops?

HireVue says you can pick up where you left off using the same link. If that does not work, contact the employer's recruiter.

## Can I ask for adjustments?

Yes. HireVue says employers may support neurodivergent candidates and people with disabilities who prefer another way to take the interview or assessment, and that you can request accommodations from your recruiter or the hiring team. If you choose to disclose that you are neurodivergent, you may be able to ask for the questions in advance.

## Who sees my video, and can I have it deleted?

The employer you applied to. HireVue says data is kept for a period chosen by that employer and then deleted, and that deletion requests go to the employer, not to HireVue.
