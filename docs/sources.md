## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not HireVue statements, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the STAR time splits and words-per-minute estimates
- the "read, rank, respond, follow up" method for situational questions
- the three-part "why us" method and story bank
- per-item timing targets for game practice
- the study plans and checklists
- all practice items, worked examples and explanations, which are original

## What is not published

- the names and number of HireVue games
- any score scale or pass mark
- fixed think times, answer times or retake numbers, which each employer sets
