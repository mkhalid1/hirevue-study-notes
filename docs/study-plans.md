# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation has already arrived. Both assume you start with the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Expect about 45 to 75 minutes a day on the 7-day plan.

First, check your invitation. If it only mentions a video interview, skip the games days and use them for extra recorded answers. If it mentions games or "assessments", keep them in.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read [HireVue explained](test-overview.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Read the job advert and list the three or four tasks it mentions most. |
| 2 | Story bank | Read [past-behaviour questions](sections/video-past-behaviour-questions.md). Do the 12 items. Write six to eight STAR stories as bullet notes: problem solved, conflict, pressure, mistake, customer or colleague helped, learning fast, leading or influencing, changing your mind. |
| 3 | Judgement and simulations | Read [situational questions](sections/video-situational-questions.md). Do the 12 items. Record two answers, one hypothetical and one acted-out simulation, and watch them back. |
| 4 | Why this job | Research the employer for an hour. Read [motivation and strengths questions](sections/video-motivation-questions.md), do the items, and write your three "why us" sentences and your strength with an example. Record both. |
| 5 | Games | Read the [numerical](sections/games-numerical.md), [memory and attention](sections/games-memory-and-attention.md) and [pattern and logic](sections/games-pattern-and-logic.md) guides. Do all three item sets against a timer. |
| 6 | People games and a full run | Read [people and work-style games](sections/games-people-and-work-style.md). Then do a full timed run, such as the [free HireVue practice on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/hirevue?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=hirevue), with games and video. Note your weakest part. |
| 7 | Setup, polish and rest | Read [setup and recording](setup-and-recording.md) and set up your room. Record your three most likely questions once more. Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Stop by early evening. |

### How to review a recorded answer

Watch it back once with the sound on and once reading only your notes. Ask three questions:

1. **Did I answer the question asked?** Including both parts of a two-part question.
2. **Could someone tell what I personally did?** Count the "I" sentences in the action part.
3. **Did I end with a result and stop cleanly?** Or did I trail off?

For game items you missed, write one line: what the rule was and what you did instead. The same two or three mistakes usually repeat.

## 2-day plan

For when the invitation has arrived and the deadline is close.

### Day 1: prepare your material

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). |
| 2 | 30 min | Read the job advert and the employer's careers page. Write your three "why us" sentences. |
| 3 | 40 min | Read the method parts of the three video guides. Write five STAR stories as bullet notes. |
| 4 | 30 min | If games are included, do the item sets for your two weakest game guides. |
| 5 | 20 min | Record two practice answers with the [free timed video practice on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/hirevue/video-interview-practice?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=hirevue) and review them with the three questions above. |

### Day 2: sit it

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 min | Set up your room using [setup and recording](setup-and-recording.md). |
| 2 | 15 min | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Put your story notes and "why us" sentences next to the screen. |
| 3 | 10 min | Use HireVue's own practice questions as a tech check and warm-up. |
| 4 | 45 min | Sit the real interview, with time to spare before the deadline. |

If you only have one evening, do day 1 blocks 2, 3 and 5, then sit it the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not memorise a script word for word.** Bullet notes sound natural. Read scripts sound read.
- **Do not hunt for "the HireVue questions".** HireVue says each employer selects its own questions, so lists online will not match yours.
- **Do not leave it to the last hour.** Technical problems happen, and the recruiter needs time to help.
- **Do not try to guess what work-style games "want".** Answer as yourself.
